What It Does
From idea selection to postmortem
Topic discoveryRuns a daily scan for AI learning, product, creator, and market signals, then dedupes them against the existing trend database.
Platform-native planningLets X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Xiaohongshu, and Pinterest each follow their own content logic instead of forcing one topic everywhere.
Approval gateRequires content approval before publishing while keeping the production work automated after the green light.
Analytics feedbackReads performance data back into topic scoring so future recommendations improve with actual audience behavior.

Why It Matters
Marketing system, not one-off posting
